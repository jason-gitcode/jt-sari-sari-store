Operating Modes

Control how the storefront behaves for customers right now.
🔒 Store Closed OFF
Stops checkout. Customers can browse but can’t place orders. Auto-closes outside Operating Hours (set in Store Info); toggling here overrides the auto state for the current period only — it resets at the next opening or closing boundary.
🌧️ Rain Mode OFF
Shows a rain notice on the storefront — useful during heavy weather when delivery may be delayed.
🛠️ Maintenance Mode OFF
Shows a maintenance screen instead of the storefront. Use when making changes that customers shouldn’t see.
📦 Offline Mode OFF
Disables online ordering at checkout while leaving the storefront browseable. Use during scheduled maintenance, inventory restocking, or any time you need to pause online orders while keeping the physical store open. Customers see a notice with your store address.

Service & Payment

Control whether you offer delivery and which payment methods customers can pick at checkout.
🛵 Offer delivery OFF
When off, the checkout hides the delivery / pickup picker and the address fields, treats every order as pickup, and disables Cash on Delivery automatically.
🏬 Offer pick-up OFF
When off, the checkout hides the pick-up option and the "Cash at pickup" payment method. At least one of delivery or pick-up must stay enabled.
💵 Accept Cash (at pickup) OFF
Pay-in-person at the store. Only relevant for pickup orders.
📱 Accept GCash OFF
GCash transfer. Available for both pickup and delivery. Configure your account name + number in Store Info.
🛵 Accept Cash on Delivery OFF
Pay-on-receipt for delivery orders. Auto-disabled if "Offer delivery" is off, regardless of this toggle.
Maximum order amount for COD (PHP)
Cash on Delivery is auto-hidden once a customer's cart reaches this amount — they're nudged to GCash instead. Set to 0 to allow COD on any order amount.
₱
📅 Schedule delivery slots OFF
Let customers pick a delivery time window at checkout. When off, delivery orders skip the slot picker entirely.
Delivery time windows
Each window is shown as a button to your customers at checkout. Add as many as you offer.
💰 Charge a delivery fee OFF
When off, delivery is always free for every order. When on, customers pay the amount below (still subject to the free-delivery threshold).
Delivery fee amount (PHP)
Charged on delivery orders that don't qualify for free delivery.

🎁 Free delivery on large orders OFF
Waive the delivery fee when a customer's cart reaches a minimum amount. Configure the threshold below.
Minimum order for free delivery (PHP)
Customers see "Free delivery on orders min. Php X" in the storefront ticker.
₱
📍 Show delivery areas OFF
List of subdivisions or barangays you serve. Shown as "Delivering to: ..." in the storefront ticker.
Delivery areas served
Each entry appears in your storefront ticker.

Appearance

Customise how the admin console looks. Changes apply instantly.
Theme
Match the system preference, or force light / dark.
Storefront layout
How customers see your products. Grid lays products out as visual cards in columns — good when the image carries the sell. List shows full-width rows with the item description visible — better for restaurants and carinderias where people need to read what's on offer. Switch any time.
Show storefront info ticker
The scrolling bar at the top of your storefront that shows your operating hours, delivery areas, free-delivery threshold, schedules, and mode badges (Rain Mode, Closed, Maintenance). Turn off to hide it entirely — useful if you want a cleaner storefront or your customers already know the info.
Category display order
Reorder how categories appear on your storefront — in the sidebar chips (grid layout) and as section headings (list layout). Use the arrows to move a category up or down. Categories from new products are auto-added at the bottom; categories with no products fall off the list automatically.
