Common questions.

Everything we get asked before signing up. If yours isn't here, the team is one message away after you start.

Is it really free? What's the catch?
PabiliMart Free is permanently free — no trial that flips to a charge later. You get a working storefront and admin panel for up to 5 products, with "Powered by Pabili Mart" in the footer. Growth raises the product cap and adds analytics; Pro additionally removes the branding and lets you point your own domain at the store — but only when you're ready.
How does GCash payment work?
Your customers see your GCash account name and number at checkout. They send payment from their phone with a reference code, then upload the receipt screenshot. You confirm the payment from your admin panel before fulfilling the order. We don't touch the money — it goes directly from customer to you.
What if I want to cancel or switch plans?
Switch up or down anytime from your admin's Billing tab. Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period, so you don't lose what you already paid for. Your storefront stays online on the Free plan even after cancelling a paid plan — your products and orders are never deleted.
What happens to my products if I downgrade?
Downgrades ask you to pick which products to retain up to the new plan's cap — your other products are archived (still in your admin, but hidden from your storefront), never deleted. The same applies in all directions: Growth → Free (choose 5 to keep shoppable), Pro → Growth (choose 500), Pro → Free (choose 5). You can swap which products are visible anytime, and if you upgrade again the full catalog comes back online. Orders and customer data are always preserved regardless of plan.
Do I need a website or technical skills to use this?
No. Sign in with your Google account, type your store name and GCash details, and you're live. Adding products, handling orders, and adjusting your delivery fee are all done from the admin panel — no coding, no plugins, no separate hosting.
Can I use my own domain (e.g. mystore.com)?
Free and Growth plans run on pabilimart.com/your-store/. PabiliMart Pro lets you point a domain you already own at your storefront so customers see mystore.com instead. We help you with the DNS setup when you upgrade.
How long does the setup take?
About 2 minutes. The Google sign-in is a few seconds, the store details form takes about a minute, and your storefront is live the moment you submit. We pre-load a starter pack of common store items so you have something to test with — keep them, edit them, or replace them entirely.
Setup Guide · 8 Hakbang

Paano gumawa ng sarili mong online store.

Sundan lang ang 8 madaling hakbang. Walang bayad, walang credit card — pagkatapos, live na ang tindahan mo online.

1
Hakbang 01 · Simulan

I-tap ang “Start your store free”

  • Buksan ang pabilimart.com sa phone o computer mo.
  • Sa home page, hanapin ang dark na button na “Start your store free” at i-tap ito.
✅ Walang bayad. Walang kailangang credit card — live ang store mo in 2 minutes.
2
Hakbang 02 · Mag-sign in

Gumawa ng account

  • I-tap ang Continue with Google — ito ang pinaka-mabilis na paraan.
  • O kaya, mag-type ng email at gumawa ng password (6+ characters), tapos i-tap ang Create account.
💡 May account ka na? I-tap ang “Have an account? Sign in.”
3
Hakbang 03 · Google sign-in

Piliin ang Google account mo

  • Ilagay ang email or phone na naka-link sa Google mo, tapos i-tap ang Next.
  • Sundan ang Google steps. Awtomatiko kang babalik sa Pabili Mart pagkatapos.
⚠️ Kung email + password ang ginamit mo, laktawan ang hakbang na ito.
4
Hakbang 04 · Pangalanan

Ilagay ang pangalan ng store

  • Sa Store name, i-type ang pangalan ng tindahan mo — ito ang makikita ng customers.
  • Awtomatikong gagawa ng Store URL. Pwede mong i-edit — pero permanent na ito kapag na-save.
💡 URL rule: lowercase letters, numbers, at hyphens lang — 3 hanggang 32 characters.
5
Hakbang 05 · Store details

Punan ang details at GCash

  • Store address — kung saan pwedeng mag-pickup ang customers.
  • GCash name at phone number (11 digits, simula sa 09) — dito magpapadala ng bayad ang customers.
  • Invite code ay optional — iwanang blangko. Tapos i-tap ang Create my store.
6
Hakbang 06 · Live na!

Ready na ang store mo

  • May 2 links ka: ang Storefront (i-share sa customers) at ang Admin (para i-manage).
  • Pre-loaded na ng 5 starter products — pwede mong i-edit o dagdagan. I-tap ang Open my admin.
📌 I-save ang storefront link — ito ang i-share mo sa mga suki para makapag-order.
7
Hakbang 07 · Admin

Ito ang Admin Dashboard mo

  • Dito makikita ang sales stats mo: products, orders, confirmed, pending, at iba pa.
  • Gamitin ang menu sa ibaba — Products para magdagdag ng items, Orders para sa orders ng customer.
✅ I-tap ang green na Storefront button para makita ang nakikita ng customers.
8
Hakbang 08 · Storefront

Ito ang nakikita ng customers

  • Eto na ang live storefront mo — kumpleto na ng products at presyo.
  • I-share ang link sa Facebook, Messenger, o sa mga suki para makapag-order na agad.
🎉 Tapos ka na! Live na ang tindahan mo online.
