Common questions.

Everything we get asked before signing up. If yours isn't here, the team is one message away after you start.

Is it really free? What's the catch?
PabiliMart Free is permanently free — no trial that flips to a charge later. You get a working storefront and admin panel for up to 5 products, with "Powered by Pabili Mart" in the footer. The paid plans remove the branding, raise the product cap, and add analytics — but only when you're ready.
How does GCash payment work?
Your customers see your GCash account name and number at checkout. They send payment from their phone with a reference code, then upload the receipt screenshot. You confirm the payment from your admin panel before fulfilling the order. We don't touch the money — it goes directly from customer to you.
What if I want to cancel or switch plans?
Switch up or down anytime from your admin's Billing tab. Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period, so you don't lose what you already paid for. Your storefront stays online on the Free plan even after cancelling a paid plan — your products and orders are never deleted.
Do I need a website or technical skills to use this?
No. Sign in with your Google account, type your store name and GCash details, and you're live. Adding products, handling orders, and adjusting your delivery fee are all done from the admin panel — no coding, no plugins, no separate hosting.
Can I use my own domain (e.g. mystore.com)?
Free and Growth plans run on pabilimart.com/your-store/. PabiliMart Pro lets you point a domain you already own at your storefront so customers see mystore.com instead. We help you with the DNS setup when you upgrade.
How long does the setup take?
About 2 minutes. The Google sign-in is a few seconds, the store details form takes about a minute, and your storefront is live the moment you submit. We pre-load a starter pack of common sari-sari items so you have something to test with — keep them, edit them, or replace them entirely.
